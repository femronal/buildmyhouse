# Start a repair | BuildMyHouse

A step-by-step repair request for one job at a family home.

**URL:** https://buildmyhouse.app/start/repair

Tell us what needs fixing, where the property is, and when a visit works.

## What happens next

1. A BuildMyHouse agent confirms the scope with you on WhatsApp.
2. You get a written scope and a staged quote in the BuildMyHouse app.
3. You approve and pay for stage 1.

Platform service fee for repairs: **₦0 (free for now)**.
