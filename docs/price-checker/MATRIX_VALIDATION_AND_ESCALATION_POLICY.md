# Price Checker — Matrix Validation & Optional Escalation Policy

**Version:** 2.0 · **Date:** 2026-07-28 · **Supersedes:** `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW_POLICY.md` v1.0 (renamed — the old title incorrectly suggested professional review was mandatory)
**Source of truth:** `apps/backend/src/price-intelligence/taxonomy/matrix.ts` (validation, confidence states, escalation) + `review.ts` (optional review records)

## Founder decision (2026-07-28)

The Price Checker operates as **scalable SaaS software, not a human-dependent consultancy**. Mandatory human professional review is **removed** from Stage 2 and from ordinary runtime flows. Matrix quality is assured by, in order:

1. **AI-generated matrices by default** — a temporary specification matrix is generated by the approved OpenAI reasoning model (env `PRICE_CHECKER_MATRIX_MODEL`, currently `gpt-5.6-sol`, referred to in project direction as GPT-5.6) before any live price search.
2. **Deterministic validation underneath** — strict schema validation (`validateTemporaryMatrix`), registered-conversion whitelisting (`validateProposedConversions` — AI may never invent factors), family-template enrichment (`enrichMatrixFromFamily`), and readiness gating (`decideReadiness`).
3. **Confidence-based handling of uncertainty** — one of seven explicit states; paid research never silently proceeds on an uncertain matrix (see `DYNAMIC_MATRIX_POLICY.md`).
4. **Optional human escalation only** — a specialist look may be *offered*, never required, for exceptional cases.
5. **No human review as a Stage 2 gate** — Stage 2 closure does not depend on recruiting or collecting professional reviews (`isHumanReviewRequired()` is hard-coded `false` and test-enforced).

## When escalation is offered (all optional — `assessEscalation`)

- Structural or safety-sensitive decisions
- Unusual industrial equipment
- Conflicting technical specifications
- Extremely high-value purchases
- Unclear or contradictory seller descriptions
- Products where incorrect identification could create safety risks
- User-requested human verification
- The AI explicitly reports it cannot produce a defensible comparison

A professional review is **not** required for normal catalogue searches, normal custom searches, or ordinary matrix generation.

## Escalation ownership (formerly "reviewer assignments")

Each family's `reviewers` field now names the professional discipline that handles *optional* escalations for that family (QS for units/BOQ questions, structural engineer for reinforcement/concrete, electrical engineer for cables/protection/inverter systems, building-services professional for plumbing/pumps/tanks, architect/finishing specialist for tiles/doors/kitchens, security/low-voltage specialist for CCTV). This is routing metadata, not a sign-off requirement.

## When an optional review does happen (record integrity)

`ProfessionalReviewRecord` and `matrixReviewStatus()` remain in force so records are honest: a matrix counts as `professionally_reviewed` only with a complete record for the *current* matrix version, an approving outcome, and an admin approver. A reviewer's name alone is not a review; matrix edits demote prior approvals to `review_outdated`. **No fake professional approval can be recorded**, and an AI spot check is never recorded as professional certification.

## Safety-sensitive wording (informational, not a gate)

Reports for safety-sensitive families (`SAFETY_SENSITIVE_FAMILIES`) may include:

> "Price research only. Product suitability and installation requirements should be confirmed by an appropriately qualified professional."

This warning must not convert ordinary searches into mandatory human-review workflows.

## Terminology validation (replaces human spot checks)

Terminology spot checks are performed by GPT-5.6, not human reviewers: real Nigerian listing texts per family are analysed against the family definition via `scripts/price-checker-terminology-check.ts` with strict structured-output validation. See `DYNAMIC_MATRIX_POLICY.md` § Terminology spot checks and `scripts/data/terminology-check-results.json`.

## Stage 4 consumption (added 2026-07-30)

The Stage 4 live-research pipeline consumes matrices through the same deterministic gate: `decideReadiness` must return `mayProceed: true` before any search spend, and the matrix summary (canonical name, family, required attributes, preferred comparison unit, service flag) is what the search planner and extractor receive. The extractor may never add facts the retrieved page does not support — its output is re-validated by `research/extraction-schema.ts` (source-URL grounding + verbatim-span price grounding) before storage. See `STAGE4_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` and ADR-0001.

## Legal boundary (unchanged)

Neither an AI check nor an optional professional review is a legal certification unless a separate formal engagement explicitly says so. Report copy must never imply certification.
